# Folk (built-in token pack): provenance

The 16 people tokens in `builtin_packs/folk/` are assembled by `tools/roster/build_folk.gd` from two
free downloads by Quaternius. Nothing else went in: no repainted textures, no AI-made content. Skin
and hair colour are material tints (the packs ship one skin tone and grey hair cards, which their own
shader tints); the arms are lowered from the T-pose to an A-pose in the build.

Obtained 2026-11-01 (a licence can change after a download, so the date is part of the record).

## Sources and licence

Both packs say CC0 on their pack page (the page's License row links the CC0 1.0 text) and in the
`License_Standard.txt` inside the download, which reads:

```
License:
CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)
Public Domain Dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Models by @Quaternius
```

Neither page nor file names the newer QAL licence. Checked the day of the download, 2026-11-01.

| Universal Base Characters | https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html | https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters, file "Universal Base Characters[Standard].zip" (the free Standard version, 128968391 bytes), through the page's own "No thanks, just take me to the downloads" (name your own price, $0) |
| Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy | https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html | https://quaternius.itch.io/modular-character-outfits-fantasy, file "Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy[Standard].zip" (294347394 bytes), same route |

The pages describe 26 and 74 models; the free Standard downloads hold a part of them (their own
`License_Standard.txt` says so): two bodies (Superhero_Male, Superhero_Female), six hairstyles
(Beard, Buns, Buzzed, BuzzedFemale, Long, SimpleParted) plus eyebrows, and four outfits (Male and
Female, Peasant and Ranger) with extra colourways (Peasant 2, Ranger 3). The paid "Source" versions
were not used.

## What went into each figure

Every figure: the head cut from the base body of its sex (triangles bound to the neck and head
bones; the packs' readme says only the head is needed under an outfit), that body's eyes and
eyebrows, every part of its outfit file except the hood and the pauldron unless the recipe asks for
them, a hairstyle (none under a hood), an optional beard. The recipe list, with skin and hair tints,
is `tools/roster/folk_recipes.gd`.

## What the build changes

- Arms lowered about 64 degrees from the T-pose and baked into static vertices (no skeleton ships);
  the clavicles take 15 percent of the turn.
- Triangles reduced (outfit to 30 percent, skin and hair to 60 percent, meshoptimizer through
  `SurfaceTool.generate_lod`); parts merged into one surface per material.
- Textures: outfit atlas 1024 px, skin 512, hair 256, eyes 128, lossy WebP at quality 0.8; normal and
  ORM maps dropped; material roughness 1, metallic 0, base colour tinted for skin and hair.
- Scaled by 0.95 (the male source is 1.82 m); the figures stand 1.69 to 1.77 m, feet on y = 0. The
  GLBs are read at native scale (1 unit = 1 m, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`), so no manifest field is needed.
- Written by `tools/roster/folk_glb.gd` (one node, one mesh, EXT_texture_webp, copyright string in
  the asset block), because Godot's own exporter writes an unused tangent array per vertex.
- Icons: `tools/roster/render_icons.gd`, 128 px, the game camera's angle, own light.
